# Business Requirements Specification – FloodNow

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-1 | Forecast tomorrow's daily mean discharge at a gauge from the gauge's own recent history. |
| BR-2 | Provide 90 % prediction intervals and report their coverage in high-flow regimes and on flood days, not only on average. |
| BR-3 | Evaluate an alert rule (upper bound above a flood threshold) against a plain point-forecast rule. |
| BR-4 | Report real-data results only; make no claim about flash-flood warning (daily data cannot resolve it). |

Out of scope: rainfall or radar inputs, sub-daily forecasting, inundation mapping, operational warning.
